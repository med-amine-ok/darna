# BLADUS logo: brief, research and concept longlist

## 1. Brief

| | |
|---|---|
| Name | **BLADUS** (from Algerian Arabic *blad*, "country, homeland"; *bladi* = "my country") |
| What it does | A marketplace to discover and book Algeria: homes, hotels and hostels, experiences, vehicle rentals and agency trips |
| Audience | Algerians travelling inside the country, the diaspora coming home, some foreign visitors; hosts and agencies on the supply side |
| Promise | "A warm way to discover Algeria: verified hosts, prices in DZD and a local team that answers." |
| Adjectives | **local / rooted**, **welcoming**, **trustworthy**, **clear**, **open (whole country)** |
| Must work | 16 px favicon and app icon, navbar (38 px), RTL Arabic pages, white canvas, navy dark surfaces, one-colour print, stamps on receipts |
| Colours (fixed) | Navy ink `#152C52`, action blue `#0B57D0`, sky blue `#3FA5E2`, white. No orange or gold (the reason the old mark clashed) |
| Type (fixed) | Fraunces (display serif), Plus Jakarta Sans (UI) |
| Rejected so far | The illustrated arch with sun, castle and road; the four arch / pin / B-in-arch / line-arch sketches |

Assumptions: the mark must not lean on religious symbols (eight-point Rub el Hizb star, crescent) or national symbols (flag, Maqam Echahid), and must not tie BLADUS to one region or one product (homes only).

## 2. Category research

Travel and booking marks (Airbnb, Booking.com, Expedia, Vrbo, Tripadvisor, Agoda, GetYourGuide) and local Maghreb travel brands.

Clichés, off-limits unless given a fresh form:
- Travel in general: globes, planes, suitcases, map pins, compass needles, suns over a horizon, roofs and houses, keys, swooshes.
- Algeria and the Maghreb: keyhole or Moorish arches (the old BLADUS mark), crescent and star, palm trees, camels, dunes drawn literally, Maqam Echahid, flag colours.

What the category does: the big brands are mostly wordmarks or a single abstract symbol with one rounded idea (Airbnb's loop, Tripadvisor's eyes). Pictorial scenes are rare at the top because they die at 16 px; that was the old mark's problem.

Opening for BLADUS: nobody in the space owns **the shape of Algeria itself**, and nobody uses **Amazigh geometric patterns** (the rhombus, chevron and "eye" motifs of Kabyle pottery, rugs and tattoos), which are shared across the whole country and carry no religious meaning.

## 3. Word map

| Name / promise | Nouns | Metaphors | Opposite to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| blad = country | the country's outline, borders, the whole map | "the whole country in one tile" | one city, one region |
| welcoming | door, threshold, open hand, cup of tea | "the door is open" | gate, lock |
| discover | horizon, path, compass, crossroads | "where sea meets land" | dead end |
| rooted / local | Amazigh motifs, zellige, rug patterns, rhombus | "a pattern every grandmother knows" | foreign, generic tech |
| trustworthy / clear | solid shape, symmetry, straight lines | "a stamp of approval" | wobbly, illustrative |

Intersections circled: **country outline + open/discover**, **letter B + horizon**, **Amazigh rhombus + compass/four products**.
